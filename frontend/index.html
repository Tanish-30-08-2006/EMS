WHAT'S INSIDE

Everything your HR team needs

Full Employee Registry
Add, search, update and delete employees. Track salary history, department transfers, and reporting structures.
Automatic Audit Trail
Every INSERT, UPDATE, and DELETE is logged automatically via PostgreSQL triggers. See exactly what changed, when, and what it looked like before.
Isolated Company Workspaces
Each company gets its own private PostgreSQL schema. Company A can never see Company B's data — guaranteed at the database level.
One-Click Data Exports
Export everything as a CSV bundle, Excel workbook (.xlsx), or JSON snapshot. Optionally email the export directly to your registered company address.
HOW IT WORKS

1
