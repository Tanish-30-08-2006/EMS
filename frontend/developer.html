I built EMS because I wanted to see what a real database system looks like end-to-end — not just the SQL, not just the UI, but the whole thing together. Turns out there are a lot of decisions: how do you isolate tenants? How do you audit every change without relying on developers to remember? How do you design a frontend that makes sense of six related tables at once?

This project is my answer to those questions. It's built to work, not just to demo.
